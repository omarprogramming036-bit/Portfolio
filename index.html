01 — About

Curiosity became
a way of building.

I'm Omar Script, a self-taught developer who started programming from zero.

My journey started with a simple question: whenever I saw something powered by technology, I wanted to know how it was made and what technology made it possible.

I started with web development and quickly built a strong foundation in JavaScript. From there, I expanded into TypeScript, Python, C++, Rust, React, Node.js, Three.js and many other technologies.

But I don't like using technology as a black box. Before depending on a technology, I prefer to build a smaller version of it and understand its strengths, weaknesses and fundamentals.

Learn deeply.
Build freely.

Technology becomes more powerful when you understand what happens underneath the abstraction.

My Philosophy

Don't just use it.
Understand it.

I learn technology by rebuilding small versions of it, experimenting with its limitations, and then using what I learned to create something real.
